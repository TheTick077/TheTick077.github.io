A regular wash is really just to get surface dirt and grime off while a detail is a very detailed process that gets all that stuck on grime as well as protecting and restoring natural shine on every surface.

While automatic car washes are convenient and quick, they can damage the paint on your car. Touchless washes are better than the ones that use brushes, but they often use extremely harsh chemicals which can mar your paint and leave lasting marks. At Refined Mobile Detailing, we approach detailing by using the safest, gentlest chemicals and techniques that will not damage the paint and will get the job done.

We don't use a wax because we have found that a hydrophobic paint sealant is actually a better way to add a protective layer to your paint while at the same time creating a glossy surface that water beads on.
