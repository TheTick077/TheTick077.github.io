Our Story

We are Justin and Luke, a father and son that love cars. All cars. Big ones, small ones, fast ones, ones that just get you from A to B, and everything in between. While learning to maintain and fix the family cars we also discovered we love to make them shine. And now we want to share that passion with you.

While a shiny clean car is the goal, ultimately we want you to be happy. Which is why we have a 100% satisfaction guarantee with every detail. Before we start a detail we will look at the car with you and make sure we understand the specific areas that you are concerned with. After we are done we will walk you through everything that we did, and make sure we address any questions or concerns you may have about your detail.

We bring the clean to you. You don't even have to leave the comfort of your couch, or arrange your entire day around getting a clean car.

Our philosophy is to treat every car as if it's our own, and every customer as our most important. We really care not only about the cars, but we care about you as well. We strive to be an example of kindness and joy through our hard work, honest communication, and conduct. We look forward to serving you and showing you how good having a clean car can feel.

Why name it Refined Mobile Detailing?

The name has a double meaning. The word refined not only reflects the kind of meticulous work we do but also how we are refined by God through our faith in Jesus.
